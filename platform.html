How it all works

How do I get started?

Create an account with Google, your email or your phone number. Then pick a plan, choose your broker, register there through our link and submit your broker account ID.

Why are my signals locked?

Signals stay locked until an admin verifies your broker account. We check that the ID you submitted is real and registered through our link. You’ll see the status (pending, verified or rejected) on your plan page and dashboard.

Automated or semi-automated: what’s the difference?

On Pocket Option you copy our master trade ID in one click, and your stake is matched to your plan. On Quotex, IQ Option and other brokers, a live signal window pops up with the pair, direction, countdown and your stake, and you tap Execute yourself.

How do the plans size my entry?

Passive uses a fixed 5–10% of your capital, your choice. Compounding steps 10% → 20% → 30% of your current balance after each win, then returns to 10%, and any loss resets it to 10%. The amount is calculated for you.

When do I pay, and how much?

Only on profit, and only after you withdraw: 20% on Passive, 40% on Compounding, 30% on Power Share. There is no subscription and nothing upfront. You pay in USDT on the TRON (TRC-20) network from your dashboard.

What is the $50 limit?

If unpaid fees reach $50, your account is suspended and signals stop until you pay. Paying brings you back under the limit and signals resume.

What is loss credit?

A share of your verified losses, equal to your plan’s fee rate, is credited against fees you owe.

Real versus demo accounts?

Fees apply to profit on real accounts. Demo trades carry no commission.

What is Power Share?

A managed pool by invitation. You own a share percentage of one master balance and your value moves with it.

What are the risks?

Binary options are high risk and you can lose your entire stake on every trade. Only trade money you can afford to lose. They are restricted or banned in some countries, so check your local rules.
